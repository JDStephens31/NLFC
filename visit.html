A morning in the hills

No surprises

What a Sunday looks like

You'll be welcomed with open arms whether you've been in church your whole life or you're visiting for the very first time.

  • You'll be greeted, not cornered. Someone will say hello and point you toward a seat. That's it — no spotlight, no standing up in front of everyone.
  • Wear whatever you own. Jeans and boots or Sunday best, both fit right in.
  • Music and a message. We sing together, we pray together, and Pastor Will brings a message grounded in God's Word and practical for everyday life.
  • Giving is for our church family. If you're visiting, consider yourself our guest — you're under no obligation whatsoever.
  • Stay a while afterward. Some of the best conversations happen in the parking lot.

Good questions

Things people ask us

What should I wear?
Whatever you're comfortable in. You'll see everything from work clothes to Sunday best on a given morning, and nobody's counting. Come as you are — we mean that.
What about my kids?
Bring them. We love seeing kids laughing and teenagers finding purpose, and we believe church is at its best when every generation worships together. If you'd rather they be in the service with you, that's completely fine too — a little noise has never bothered anybody here.
How long is the service?
Plan on about an hour and fifteen minutes for Sunday morning worship, with time before and after to meet people. Wednesday Bible study usually runs about an hour.
Will I be asked to give money or sign anything?
No. Giving is a way our church family supports the work here, and guests are never expected to participate. There's no pressure to sign up for anything on your first visit — or your tenth.
I'm not sure what I believe. Am I still welcome?
Absolutely. Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or you're looking for a church to call home, there's a seat here for you. No matter where you've been or what your story looks like, there's hope, healing, and a fresh start in Jesus.
Can I talk to the pastor?
Yes — Pastor Will would genuinely love to meet you. Catch him after the service, or call ahead and he'll make a point of finding you.

One more thing

Tell us you're coming

It's completely optional — but if you give us a call, we'll watch for you at the door so you're never walking in alone.
