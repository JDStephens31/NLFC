Will and I have a heart for serving God and His people. I accepted Christ as my Savior as a young child, and my walk with the Lord has been the foundation of my life ever since.
In 1999, my mom and stepdad founded a church where I had the privilege of serving in many areas of ministry, including the nursery, media ministry, and the church office. Those years taught me the importance of faithfulness and the value of the ministry of helps.

Will and I were married in 2002 and have been blessed with three wonderful sons. Throughout our marriage, we have continued serving together wherever God has called us, believing that every role in the body of Christ is significant.

Today, we are honored to answer God's call by planting a church in Campton, Kentucky. Our desire is to share the love of Jesus, teach the truth of God's Word, and build a church where people can encounter Christ, grow in their faith, and find a loving church family. We are grateful for all God has done and look forward to what He will continue to do in our community.
