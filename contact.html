Need prayer?

Call us and ask for prayer — it goes straight to our pastoral team and stays between us. You don't have to be a member, and you don't have to explain everything.

Before you go

A few quick answers

How fast will you reply?

If we can't pick up, leave a message and we'll call you back — usually within a day or two.

Can I talk to the pastor?

Yes — Pastors Will and Stephanie would genuinely love to meet you. Catch them after a service, or call ahead and they'll make a point of finding you.

Want to serve?

There's a place for you. Give us a call and tell us a little about what you enjoy doing, and we'll help you find where you fit.
