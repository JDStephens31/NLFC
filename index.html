Welcome home

A church that feels like family

Our vision is simple: we want to see people's lives get better by discovering who they are in Christ.

No matter where you've been or what your story looks like, there's hope, healing, and a fresh start in Jesus. We believe He has a purpose for every person who walks through our doors.

We love seeing kids laughing, teenagers finding purpose, parents growing stronger, and grandparents passing their faith on to the next generation. Church is at its best when every generation worships, serves, and grows together.

Meet our pastors

Pastors Will & Stephanie Stephens

A heart for serving God and His people — together since 2002, now planting a church in Campton.

Alongside their three sons, Pastors Will and Stephanie are committed to helping people experience the love of Jesus, grow in their faith, and find a place to belong.

  • A lifetime in Eastern Kentucky. Born and raised in Wolfe County, Pastor Will knows both the joys and the challenges of small-town life, and considers it a privilege to serve the community he has always called home.
  • Servants' hearts. Between them: children's and youth ministry, nursery, media, church administration and the church office — they learned the value of serving wherever God opened a door.
  • A passion for transformation. Their greatest joy is seeing lives changed by the Gospel.

Three steps to your first Sunday

1

Let us know you're coming

Optional, but it helps. Give us a call and we'll watch for you at the door so you're never walking in alone.

2
